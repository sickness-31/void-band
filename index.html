The Band

About

Based in Calgary, Alberta, VO!D is a metal band taking influence from all things heavy and groovy. After years of crafting a sound that is uniquely theirs, they have recently begun to release music.

VO!D seeks to demonstrate uniquely energetic, raw and crushing riffs, groovy forward-pushing drums, and loud, harsh vocals blended into a wall of sound that moves you.

Certainly small and upcoming, VO!D's members are chomping at the bit to be heard — and love to get a room moving.

Rowan Campbell Vocals
Kyle Kristjansson Guitar
Jordan Pearton Drums
